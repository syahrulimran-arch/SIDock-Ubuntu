## Reference ligand chemistry integration

The deferred reference-selection layer now uses deterministic CCD-style graph
identity, explicit instance-to-chemistry mapping, equivalent-instance grouping,
occupancy/altloc/RSCC/RSR-aware ranking, local PDB/mmCIF fallback, explicit
override validation, candidate/selection provenance, and E101 for unresolved
low-confidence automatic selection. It is independent of docking, RMSD, PLIP,
and compound results; restart reuse and source/config invalidation remain
covered by the existing stage contracts.

Reference ranking policy v1 is now production-integrated: mapping, altloc,
occupancy, deposited RSCC/RSR, local binding-site/peripheral/contact
completeness, and a stable identifier tie-break are persisted in the candidate
audit and selection provenance. Remote enrichment remains deferred.
